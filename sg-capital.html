SG Capital Research was founded on a simple premise: that decisions built with the same rigor used to model credit risk in regulated institutions hold up better than those built on narrative alone.

We produce global macroeconomic, cross-asset and equity research — reading rates, currencies, commodities and equity markets as one interconnected system, and testing every view with quantitative strategies and scenario analysis before it reaches a client.

We work with a small number of institutional and private clients at a time, so every mandate gets senior attention and research built around its specific market exposure, time horizon and risk tolerance — not a view shared across hundreds of accounts.
